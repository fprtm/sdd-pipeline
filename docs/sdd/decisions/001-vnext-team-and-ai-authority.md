# Decision #1: Target full teams and bound AI authority

**Date**: 2026-10-02
**Status**: ACCEPTED

SDD Pipeline vNext targets full product/engineering teams while preserving an explicit small-team multi-hat degradation path. AI may research, draft, evaluate deterministic policy, and implement approved bounded work, but may not self-approve A2/A3 acceptance, security exceptions, production release, benchmark promotion, or irreversible operations.

## Alternatives Considered

- Solo-first optimization would reduce ceremony but make the role and authority boundaries advisory where vNext intends to verify them.
- Regulated-first optimization would overfit the default product to A3 costs before general-team evidence exists.
