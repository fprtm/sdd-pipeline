# Art Deco

**When to use**: Luxury hospitality, jewelry, and premium event brands wanting glamorous, geometric 1920s-30s opulence.
**Palette example**: #0B0B0B (black), #C9A227 (gold foil), #0F5C4A (emerald), #F5EFE0 (ivory bg)
**Typography**: Poiret One / Broadway (display headings), Didot (elegant body/subheads)
**Visual traits**:
- Symmetrical geometric ornamentation: sunbursts, chevrons, stepped ziggurat forms
- Thin gold linework framing content blocks, high-contrast black/gold pairing
- Elongated, elegant lettering with wide letter-spacing on headings
**Not this if**: The brand is casual, budget-friendly, or needs a modern minimal tone.

For deeper technique detail (exact CSS/animation implementation), web-search for "Art Deco design CSS gold geometric ornament" rather than guessing — this file gives direction, not a full spec.
