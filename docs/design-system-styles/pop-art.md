# Pop Art

**When to use**: Bold consumer brands, music/fashion campaigns, or retail wanting a loud, retro-1960s statement look.
**Palette example**: #FFED00 (pop yellow), #E30022 (pop red), #0057B7 (pop blue), #FFFFFF (bg)
**Typography**: Anton (headlines) / Helvetica Now (body)
**Visual traits**:
- Ben-Day halftone dot screens on flat color fields (Lichtenstein-style)
- Oversized bold outlined typography, often at an angle, high saturation only, no gradients
- Repetition of a single image in different flat colors (Warhol-style grid)
**Not this if**: The brand needs subtlety, minimalism, or a calm/trustworthy tone (finance, healthcare).

For deeper technique detail (exact CSS/animation implementation), web-search for "pop art CSS design" rather than guessing — this file gives direction, not a full spec.
