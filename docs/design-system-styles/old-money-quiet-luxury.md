# Old Money / Quiet Luxury

**When to use**: Heritage finance, private banking, or legacy apparel brands wanting understated, generational-wealth credibility.
**Palette example**: #F5F0E6 (cream), #0B3D2E (hunter green), #7A6A53 (taupe), #2C2C2C (charcoal text)
**Typography**: Garamond Premier (headings) / Georgia (body)
**Visual traits**:
- Muted, desaturated neutrals — no bright accents, restraint is the message
- Thin serif type, generous letter-spacing on small caps labels, monogram-style crests
- Flat, uncluttered layouts with subtle texture (linen, canvas) instead of gradients or shadows
**Not this if**: The brand needs to signal innovation, youth, or accessibility — this style reads exclusive and closed-off.
