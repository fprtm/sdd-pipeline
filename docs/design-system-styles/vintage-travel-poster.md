# Vintage Travel Poster

**When to use**: Tourism, hospitality, and destination brands wanting romantic, nostalgic golden-age-of-travel appeal.
**Palette example**: #C1440E (terracotta sunset), #1B4B5A (deep teal sea), #EDCB96 (sand cream bg), #F2A65A (warm gold)
**Typography**: Trade Gothic / Futura (headings), Baskerville (body captions)
**Visual traits**:
- Flat, layered illustration with limited color separations (mimicking lithograph printing)
- Bold destination lettering integrated into the scene, often arched or banner-shaped
- Simplified iconic landmarks/landscapes with strong horizon-line composition
**Not this if**: The brand needs to feel contemporary, tech-driven, or minimalist.

For deeper technique detail (exact CSS/animation implementation), web-search for "vintage travel poster design CSS lithograph illustration" rather than guessing — this file gives direction, not a full spec.
