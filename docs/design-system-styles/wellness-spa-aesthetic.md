# Wellness / Spa Aesthetic

**When to use**: Health, meditation, spa, or self-care brands wanting a calm, natural, restorative feel.
**Palette example**: #E8E2D6 (sand beige bg), #7C9885 (sage green), #C97C5D (terracotta clay), #FDFBF7 (off-white)
**Typography**: Fraunces (headings) / Karla (body)
**Visual traits**:
- Soft, low-contrast natural palette (sage, sand, clay, cream) evoking earth and skin tones
- Organic rounded shapes, generous whitespace, slow gentle motion/fade transitions
- Natural texture photography (linen, stone, water ripples) rather than flat vector icons
**Not this if**: The brand needs high energy, urgency, or bold conversion-driven messaging (e-commerce flash sales, gaming).

For deeper technique detail (exact CSS/animation implementation), web-search for "wellness spa aesthetic CSS design" rather than guessing — this file gives direction, not a full spec.
