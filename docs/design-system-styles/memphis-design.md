# Memphis Design

**When to use**: Playful youth brands, creative agencies, or 80s-nostalgia products wanting quirky geometric energy.
**Palette example**: #FF6F91 (pink), #00C2D1 (turquoise), #FFC93C (yellow), #000000 (outline black), #F4F1DE (cream bg)
**Typography**: Futura or Avant Garde (headings) / Helvetica (body)
**Visual traits**:
- Squiggles, zigzags, confetti dots, and terrazzo-pattern fills as decoration
- Clashing primary/pastel color combos used together intentionally
- Bold geometric shapes (triangles, circles) placed asymmetrically, often overlapping
**Not this if**: The brand needs to feel serious, minimal, or premium-luxury.
