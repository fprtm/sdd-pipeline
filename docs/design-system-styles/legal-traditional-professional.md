# Legal / Traditional Professional

**When to use**: Law firms, consultancies, and institutional finance sites wanting to project gravitas, heritage, and authority.
**Palette example**: #14213D (navy), #A08039 (muted gold accent), #FFFFFF (bg), #333333 (body text)
**Typography**: Georgia / Playfair Display (headings), Source Serif Pro (body)
**Visual traits**:
- Serif typography throughout, symmetrical layouts, centered navigation
- Restrained color palette of navy/gold/burgundy with no bright accents
- Formal photography (handshakes, offices, portraits) over illustration
**Not this if**: The audience is younger/consumer and expects modern, casual, tech-forward visuals.

For deeper technique detail (exact CSS/animation implementation), web-search for "traditional law firm website design serif typography" rather than guessing — this file gives direction, not a full spec.
