# Collage / Cut-Paper

**When to use**: Creative studios, editorial features, or campaigns wanting a handcrafted, layered, tactile feel.
**Palette example**: #F2E9DC (kraft paper bg), #D64550 (torn-edge red), #2A6F77 (teal cutout), #1A1A1A (ink outline)
**Typography**: Canela or Recoleta (headings, mixed with hand-lettering) / Inter (body)
**Visual traits**:
- Layered cutout shapes with visible torn or scissor-cut edges and drop shadows
- Mixed media: photo fragments, paper textures, and hand-drawn marks combined
- Deliberate overlap and slight rotation of elements, not grid-aligned
**Not this if**: The product needs a systematic, highly consistent component library at scale.

For deeper technique detail (exact CSS/animation implementation), web-search for "collage cut-paper web design CSS" rather than guessing — this file gives direction, not a full spec.
