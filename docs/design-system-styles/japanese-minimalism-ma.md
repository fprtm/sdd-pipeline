# Japanese Minimalism (Ma)

**When to use**: Premium, contemplative brands (design studios, tea/wellness, luxury goods) that want restraint and negative space as a feature.
**Palette example**: #F7F5F2 (washi white bg), #1C1C1C (sumi ink), #B7202E (hinomaru red accent, used once)
**Typography**: Zen Old Mincho or Noto Serif JP (headings) / Noto Sans JP or Helvetica Neue (body)
**Visual traits**:
- Deliberate empty space (ma) treated as active composition, not filler
- Asymmetric balance rather than centered symmetry
- Single accent color used at most once per screen for emphasis
**Not this if**: The product needs dense information display or high visual energy.
