# Retro-Futurism

**When to use**: Sci-fi entertainment, space/tech brands, and events wanting a "yesterday's tomorrow" atomic-age optimism.
**Palette example**: #FF6B35 (burnt orange), #F7C548 (atomic gold), #004E89 (deep space blue), #EDEDE9 (bg cream)
**Typography**: Space Grotesk / Bank Gothic (headings), Futura (body)
**Visual traits**:
- Atomic/boomerang shapes, starbursts, and swooping rocket-fin curves
- Chrome and saturated primary-color combos evoking 1950s-60s "world of tomorrow" ads
- Bold geometric iconography: satellites, ringed planets, googie architecture motifs
**Not this if**: The brand needs to feel cutting-edge contemporary rather than nostalgic.

For deeper technique detail (exact CSS/animation implementation), web-search for "retro-futurism atomic age design CSS" rather than guessing — this file gives direction, not a full spec.
