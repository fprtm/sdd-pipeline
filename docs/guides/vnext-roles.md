# vNext role accountability

[Policy] Nine canonical role contracts and their ownership, approval, blocking,
self-approval, input, output, evidence, escalation, and fallback fields live in
`skills/meta/quality-contract/rules/roles.mjs`.

[Mechanical] Exact-subject assignments, expiry, conflicts, qualifications, and
A2/A3 separation are evaluated by TEST-028. Repository actor labels record
separation but do not prove real identity.

[Runtime] Missing or conflicted required roles block; A1 multi-hat work becomes
explicitly degraded.

[Host-dependent] Human identity and qualifications require external attestation
from the organization operating the host.
